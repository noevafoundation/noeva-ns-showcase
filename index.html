WHAT I'VE BEEN BUILDING

Similar to Zcash
– for identity.

Four pieces of the architecture, in active development at Network School.

DATA

Revocability & ephemerality.

Granular, revocable consent over what you share, with who, and for how long. Nothing sticks by default.

KERNEL

Safety in the protocol.

Constraints live inside the kernel itself. No setting to switch them off – not even for me.

CRYPTO

Selective disclosure.

Zero-knowledge techniques from Zcash, applied to who you are – not what you own.

AGENTS

Multi-vendor orchestration.

Claude, Cursor, Antigravity, OpenAI and Gemini in defined lanes – with me as arbiter.
